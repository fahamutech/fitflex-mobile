# Hero photo licences

Photos behind the photo heroes (`PHOTO_HERO_UI`) and the photo footer (`PHOTO_FOOTER_UI`), docs/design/photo-hero-screens.md. **No row, no ship.**

Replaced 2026-10-22 (owner approval in chat, 2026-10-22): the interim Pexels stock is gone. Every file now comes from one of two sources:

- **Generated (FitFlex Africa).** Made with Figma AI (model gpt-image-2.5) on 2026-10-22 in the owner's Figma file "FitFlex Africa — app hero photos (AI drafts)" (key `puGVsWuNb8IJb43IkB85Hc`), owned by FitFlex. Licence entry for each: "FitFlex Africa, AI-generated via Figma AI (gpt-image-2.5), 2026-10-22, no people". The scenes are invented interiors; they are not partner gyms and must not be presented as one. Originals: parent repo `docs/design/generated/gen-*.png` (864x1536 portraits, 1024x1024 footers).
- **Owner-supplied (testing only).** Four photos the owner supplied on 2026-10-22 (parent repo `docs/design/generated/owner/*.jpg`, about 736px wide). The owner said on 2026-10-22 they were collected from the internet and are OK for testing now: they are **not cleared for release**. Licence entry: "Collected from the internet by owner; NOT cleared for release — testing only (owner 2026-10-22). Replace before PHOTO_HERO_UI goes on for users." They are listed in `kTestOnlyHeroAssets` (lib/shared/components/ff_photo_hero.dart), and `test/photo_hero_release_guard_test.dart` fails a test run with `--dart-define=PHOTO_HERO_RELEASE=true` while any of them is still mapped. Two show one man seen from behind (no face in frame; owner rule 2026-10-19: backs, hands or unclear faces only). Their maker-logo trainers (Nike) are cropped out.

Processing (2026-10-22): cropped from the original at 1:1 for the 2x file (never resized up, so every 2x file is at most as wide as its source: 864 for the portraits, 1024 for the footers, 736/732 for the owner photos), halved (Lanczos) for the 1x file, converted to RGB, WebP quality 72 method 6. Only the VP8 image chunk is written: no EXIF, XMP or ICC. The owner photos are low resolution (736/732 wide): at 2x on a 390pt phone they are shown at about 1.06x and look a little soft; the generated portraits are at about 0.9x (sharp). Owner photos `hero_trainers` and `hero_activity_challenges` are brightened x1.25 and x1.20 (they are very dark under the scrim); nothing else is graded. Exception (2026-10-22): `hero_shop` is a taller 864x1080 crop, so its 2x file is resized down (never up) to 768x960 and saved at WebP quality 60 to stay within the section band cap (60 KB at 2x, 25 KB at 1x).

Each file is its own scene (`sourceId` = the source file's name, `fitflex-gen-NN-…` or `owner-NN-…`), so no top hero shares a scene with any footer or with another hero.

Signage: the FitFlex Africa signage in the generated scenes is kept in the clear photo above the scrim where the layout allows (Language, Sign in, Create account, Role, Home, Profile, Shop, every footer), and otherwise cropped out, so no sign sits partly behind the text (owner 2026-10-22). Re-cut 2026-10-22 for the band heroes with clear photo above the text and the PIN thin strip: Shop is a 4:5 crop from the top of the source (focal top), so on a phone band the whole sign sits in the clear photo above the text; PIN (a thin strip of text under the bar), Upgrade (no room for clear photo above its plan cards), Trainer home and Vendor home (their bands keep today's height in Swahili and under 760px tall) are cropped clear of their signs and taped boxes. Activity: Training and gym owner home are cropped below their wall signs and Activity: Progress above its floor decal. No crop shows another brand's logo or signage (owner rule 2026-10-20).
