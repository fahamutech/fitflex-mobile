# Hero photo licences

Photos behind the photo heroes (`PHOTO_HERO_UI`) and the photo footer (`PHOTO_FOOTER_UI`), docs/design/photo-hero-screens.md. **No row, no ship.**

Replaced 2026-10-22 (owner approval in chat, 2026-10-22): the interim Pexels stock is gone. Every file now comes from one of two sources:

- **Generated (FitFlex Africa).** Made with Figma AI (model gpt-image-2.5) on 2026-10-22 in the owner's Figma file "FitFlex Africa — app hero photos (AI drafts)" (key `puGVsWuNb8IJb43IkB85Hc`), owned by FitFlex. Licence entry for each: "FitFlex Africa, AI-generated via Figma AI (gpt-image-2.5), 2026-10-22, no people". The scenes are invented interiors; they are not partner gyms and must not be presented as one. Originals: parent repo `docs/design/generated/gen-*.png` (864x1536 portraits, 1024x1024 footers).
- **Owner-supplied.** Four photos the owner supplied on 2026-10-22 (parent repo `docs/design/generated/owner/*.jpg`, about 736px wide). Licence entry: "Supplied by owner 2026-10-22; owner to confirm source/licence before flag on". Two show one man seen from behind (no face in frame; owner rule 2026-10-19: backs, hands or unclear faces only). Their maker-logo trainers (Nike) are cropped out.

Processing (2026-10-22): cropped from the original at 1:1 for the 2x file (never resized up, so every 2x file is at most as wide as its source: 864 for the portraits, 1024 for the footers, 736/732 for the owner photos), halved (Lanczos) for the 1x file, converted to RGB, WebP quality 72 method 6. Only the VP8 image chunk is written: no EXIF, XMP or ICC. The owner photos are low resolution (736/732 wide): at 2x on a 390pt phone they are shown at about 1.06x and look a little soft; the generated portraits are at about 0.9x (sharp). Owner photos `hero_trainers` and `hero_activity_challenges` are brightened x1.25 and x1.20 (they are very dark under the scrim); nothing else is graded.

Each file is its own scene (`sourceId` = the source file's name, `fitflex-gen-NN-…` or `owner-NN-…`), so no top hero shares a scene with any footer or with another hero.

Signage: the FitFlex Africa signage in the generated scenes is kept in the clear photo above the scrim where the layout allows (Language, Sign in, Create account, Role, PIN, Home, Profile, Shop, every footer). The band heroes (Upgrade, Trainer, Vendor, Gyms, Trainers, Shop) and the Activity heroes have text from their top edge, so a sign there sits under the scrim; Activity: Training and gym owner home are cropped below their wall signs and Activity: Progress above its floor decal, so no half-hidden sign sits behind a headline. No crop shows another brand's logo or signage (owner rule 2026-10-20).
