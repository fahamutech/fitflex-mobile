- **Generated (FitFlex Africa).** Made with Figma AI (model gpt-image-2.5) in the owner's Figma file "FitFlex Africa — app hero photos (AI drafts)" (key `puGVsWuNb8IJb43IkB85Hc`), owned by FitFlex: `gen-01`..`gen-19` on 2026-10-22, `gen-21`..`gen-24` on 2026-10-28. Licence entry for each: "FitFlex Africa, AI-generated via Figma AI (gpt-image-2.5), <date>, no people". The scenes are invented interiors; they are not partner gyms and must not be presented as one. Originals: parent repo `docs/design/generated/gen-*.png` (864x1536 portraits, 1024x1024 footers).
- **No test-only photos (2026-10-28).** The four photos the owner collected from the internet (owner 2026-10-22: OK for testing, not cleared for release) were on Gyms, Trainers, Activity: Today and Activity: Challenges. On 2026-10-28 (owner approval in chat) they were replaced by `gen-21`..`gen-24`, their files are gone, and `kTestOnlyHeroAssets` (lib/shared/components/ff_photo_hero.dart) is empty. `test/photo_hero_release_guard_test.dart` still fails a run with `--dart-define=PHOTO_HERO_RELEASE=true` if a photo is ever put back on that list, and the user builds run it whenever `PHOTO_HERO_UI` is on.

Processing (2026-10-22, and the same for the 2026-10-28 files): cropped from the original at 1:1 for the 2x file (never resized up, so every 2x file is at most as wide as its source: 864 for the portraits, 1024 for the footers), halved (Lanczos) for the 1x file, converted to RGB, WebP quality 72 method 6. Only the VP8 image chunk is written: no EXIF, XMP or ICC. Nothing is graded. The 2026-10-28 crops keep each slot's aspect (Gyms 864x487 and Trainers 864x516 for the bands, both Activity photos 3:2); Activity: Today is 810 wide so the right shoe's maker mark is out of every crop. Exception (2026-10-22): `hero_shop` is a taller 864x1080 crop, so its 2x file is resized down (never up) to 768x960 and saved at WebP quality 60 to stay within the section band cap (60 KB at 2x, 25 KB at 1x).

Each file is its own scene (`sourceId` = the source file's name, `fitflex-gen-NN-…`), so no top hero shares a scene with any footer or with another hero, and Activity's four sections show four scenes.

Signage: the FitFlex Africa signage in the generated scenes is kept in the clear photo above the scrim where the layout allows (Language, Sign in, Create account, Role, Home, Profile, Shop, every footer), and otherwise cropped out, so no sign sits partly behind the text (owner 2026-10-22). Re-cut 2026-10-22 for the band heroes with clear photo above the text and the PIN thin strip: Shop is a 4:5 crop from the top of the source (focal top), so on a phone band the whole sign sits in the clear photo above the text; PIN (a thin strip of text under the bar), Upgrade (no room for clear photo above its plan cards), Trainer home and Vendor home (their bands keep today's height in Swahili and under 760px tall) are cropped clear of their signs and taped boxes. Activity: Training and gym owner home are cropped below their wall signs and Activity: Progress above its floor decal. 2026-10-28: Trainers (the polo print) and Activity: Today (the bottle print) keep their FitFlex Africa print in the clear photo above the text on phones from 760 tall; Gyms and Activity: Challenges are cropped below their wall signs, which are as wide as or wider than the phone crop shows and would be cut at the edges. On phones under 760 tall a band has no clear photo: there the Today print sits under the scrim ramp just above the first line of text, and the Trainers print sits dimmed under the text scrim to the right of the eyebrow line (whole, not cut, and clear of the letters; checked at 360x640, English and Swahili). No crop shows another brand's logo or signage (owner rule 2026-10-20).
