# Hero photo licences

Photos behind the photo heroes (`PHOTO_HERO_UI`, docs/design/photo-hero-screens.md). Interim stock until FitFlex has its own Dar es Salaam shoot. **No row, no ship.**

All files come from the parent repo's `social/brand/photos/` (branch `release-candidate`; record in `social/brand/photos/README.md` there). They were downloaded from Pexels on 2026-10-11; each photo page showed the licence label "Free" when checked on 2026-10-16 ([Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/): free to use and modify, no attribution required). None is marked AI-generated.

Processing (2026-10-19): cropped to the hero aspect ratio, converted to sRGB, resized, re-encoded as WebP (quality 72). Metadata (EXIF, GPS, ICC) is not carried over. 1x files sit in this folder, 2x files in `2.0x/`.

No photo shows an identifiable person, so no model releases are needed. The section bands (`hero_gyms`, `hero_trainers`, `hero_shop`, spec §34.1) come from photos that show a face somewhere in the frame; each crop leaves every face out (`hero_gyms` has no person; `hero_trainers` and `hero_shop` show hands and arms only). Four addendum photos (`hero_pin`, `hero_upgrade`, `hero_activity_challenges`, `hero_trainer`) show hands, arms, a torso or a leg; each is cropped so that no face is in frame (owner decision 2026-10-19: backs, hands or unclear faces only). `hero_activity_today` (2026-10-20) shows legs, shoes and a suitcase only, cropped about 1,000px below the chin; no face in frame. No location or gym consent applies (the photos are not of FitFlex partner gyms and are not presented as such). No expiry.
